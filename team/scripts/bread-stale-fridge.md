## السكربت

**[0-3 ثوانٍ | hook]**
Putting bread in the fridge makes it go stale faster. Here is why.

**[3-15 ثانية]**
Bread goes stale mostly because of starch. When bread is baked, the starch absorbs water and turns soft. As it cools, the starch molecules slowly line up again and squeeze out the water.

**[15-32 ثانية]**
That realignment is called retrogradation. It happens fastest at cold temperatures, around the range of a fridge, not at freezing or at room heat.

**[32-42 ثانية]**
So the cold slows down mold, but it speeds up the starch change. That is why freezing works better for long storage.
