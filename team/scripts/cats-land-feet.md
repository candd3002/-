## السكربت

**[0-3 ثوانٍ | hook]**
Even when a falling cat starts upside down, it still lands on its feet.

**[3-15 ثانية]**
Cats have a built-in righting reflex. Their balance system, in the inner ear, tells the brain which way is up, and the body starts to turn.

**[15-32 ثانية]**
They do it in stages. The front half of the body twists first, and the back half follows. Their flexible spine lets them rotate with almost no angular push.

**[32-42 ثانية]**
Because the front and back halves turn at different times, the cat can bring its legs around to face the ground.
