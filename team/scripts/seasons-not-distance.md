## السكربت

**[0-3 ثوانٍ | hook]**
Earth is actually closest to the sun in January. So why is it winter in the north?

**[3-18 ثانية]**
The answer is the tilt. Earth's axis leans about 23.4 degrees compared with its orbit.

**[18-38 ثانية]**
When the north leans toward the sun, sunlight hits it more directly and the days are longer. That means more heat. When it leans away, the light comes in at a slant and spreads over more ground.

**[38-52 ثانية]**
The southern hemisphere has the opposite season at the same time for the same reason.
