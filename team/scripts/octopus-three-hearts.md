## السكربت

**[0-3 ثوانٍ | hook]**
An octopus has three hearts, and its blood is blue.

**[3-15 ثانية]**
Two of the hearts are called branchial hearts. Each one pushes blood through one set of gills, where the blood picks up oxygen from the water.

**[15-30 ثانية]**
The third heart, called the systemic heart, sends that oxygen-rich blood to the rest of the body.

**[30-40 ثانية]**
Why blue? Octopus blood uses a copper-based protein called hemocyanin to carry oxygen. Copper turns blue when it binds oxygen.
