## السكربت

**[0:00-0:25 | hook]**
A huge steel ship can sink, stop, and come back up. Not by a propeller alone, but by changing how heavy it is.

**[0:25-1:30 | الطفو]**
Any object in water pushes back with a force equal to the weight of the water it moves. If the object weighs less than that water, it floats. If it weighs more, it sinks. This is Archimedes' principle.

**[1:30-3:00 | الغاصة وخزانات الموازنة]**
A submarine has ballast tanks. To dive, it lets water in, which makes it heavier than the water it displaces. To rise, it pushes the water out with compressed air, and the total weight drops below the displaced weight.

**[3:00-4:30 | التحكم في العمق]**
Small changes in water and air matter a lot. Hydroplanes on the hull tilt the submarine up or down, so it can move forward at a steady depth without constant pumping.

**[4:30-5:30 | لماذا لا تخرج كل الغواصات بالسرعة نفسها]**
Water density changes with salt and temperature. A submarine has to correct for that, and the crew adjusts the balance constantly.

**[5:30-6:00 | خاتمة]**
The same rule that makes a boat float is the one that lets a submarine rise. Follow for more physics without the math.
