## السكربت

**[0-3 ثوانٍ | hook]**
The Romans moved water across kilometers without a single pump.

**[3-18 ثانية]**
Their secret was gravity. Water flows downhill, so an aqueduct only needs to fall slowly, from the source down to the city.

**[18-38 ثانية]**
The channel had to drop very gently. Too steep and the water rushes and damages the stone. Too flat and it stops. Engineers measured the slope carefully, often with simple levels and water.

**[38-52 ثانية]**
Where a valley blocked the way, they built arches, like the ones still standing today, to keep the channel level over the gap.
